# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a respectful, harassment-free place for everyone,
whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression,
experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept direct, specific technical feedback. Criticise code, never people.
- Back every claim with evidence: a test, a log, or a read-back.
- Respect the privacy of customers and colleagues. Never paste customer data
  or credentials into an issue, a PR or a chat.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or sexualised language and imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberate derailing.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that break this code,
and may ban anyone who breaks it, temporarily or permanently. To report a
problem, contact the maintainers privately. Reports are kept confidential.
