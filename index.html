How it works

Read. Swipe. Or revise.

Each email becomes one card. The card asks one question and shows exactly what will happen.

Step 1

Read the card

The question at the top, the customer's own words, and the reply Sayso wrote. Scroll up for earlier emails.

Step 2

Swipe to decide

Right sends it. Left skips it. The next card slides in, with no pop-ups in the way.

Or

Or revise it

Tap Revise: "Warmer", "Shorter", or your own words. Sayso rewrites it and keeps the lesson.

Swipe up for details

Everything behind the reply, one swipe away.

When a case needs a closer look, swipe up. You see what Sayso checked, what each button will do, and the full history.

✓What Sayso checked. For example: "Stripe shows 2 charges of $49.00 on 3 Oct."
✓What Send will do. "Refund $49.00 + send." Money only moves on your Yes.
✓The full conversation and an activity log of every step Sayso took.

Learning powered by Hermes Agent

Every decision makes the next draft better.

Sayso uses Hermes, an open-source agent framework, as its learning layer. Hermes turns your sends, skips and revisions into plain rules you can see and switch off.

Learns from patternsOne skip is noise. The same correction three times becomes a lesson.
You can see every ruleLessons appear in Insights in plain words, each with an on/off switch.
Never acts aloneHermes changes how replies are written. It never sends, refunds or cancels.

Questions

Who is Sayso for?

Small SaaS teams, usually 1–10 people, where the founders still answer support themselves.

What is Hermes, and does it see my data?

Hermes Agent is the open-source agent framework Sayso uses for learning. It reads your decisions so it can write better drafts. Sayso only lets it change how drafts are written: sending, refunds and cancellations always wait for a person.

Can it send without me?

No. Every customer email and every money action waits for a person.

Is the phone app ready?

Not yet. Discord approvals run today. The phone app, web app and Slack are being built in the open on GitHub.
