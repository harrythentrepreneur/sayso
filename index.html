How it works today

Four steps. A person decides at the one that matters.

This is what runs today, in the hosted web app and in Discord.

1 · Email comes in

Sayso reads new mail in your support inbox and pulls up the customer's Stripe history.

2 · AI drafts

It writes the reply, and the refund if one is due, with your own AI key. Nothing has gone out yet.

3 · You say Yes

You see the exact words and the exact amount. Yes sends it. No drops it. Edit the text and you are asked again.

4 · Sent once, proven

It goes out once. "Sent" means your Sent folder has it. "Refunded" means Stripe says so.

Questions

Who is Sayso for?

Small SaaS teams, usually 1–10 people, where the founders still answer support themselves.

Hosted or self-hosted?

Hosted (sayso.omo.sh) is $49 a month with a 14-day trial: we run the server, backups and alerts. Self-hosted is free under Apache-2.0: you run it on your own server. Both use the same core and the same safety rules.

Does my data train an AI?

No. Drafts are written with your own AI key, under your provider's terms. We do not use your customers' emails to train models. See the privacy policy.

Can it send without me?

No. Every customer email and every money action waits for a person.

Is the phone app ready?

Swiping is. Open the Sayso web app on your phone and use the Swipe tab: right for Yes, left for No. You can also approve in Discord. A native iPhone app, one-tap Revise and learning are coming next, built in the open on GitHub.
